# Korzah brand identity

## Brand idea

Korzah gives property teams a reliable structure for relationships, opportunities, and decisions. The identity combines architectural permanence with operational clarity: the tower mark is both a building and an open gateway.

**Positioning:** The bilingual CRM built for serious property operations.

**English promise:** Built for lasting relationships.

**Arabic promise:** علاقات تُبنى لتدوم.

## Personality

- Architectural, not ornamental
- Assured, not loud
- Premium, not ostentatious
- Precise, not clinical
- Local in understanding, international in execution

## Audience

Property developers, brokers, owners, sales leaders, and client-service teams in Egypt and the Gulf who need a trustworthy Arabic-first operating system.

## Logo system

The supplied `KORZAH` wordmark is the master logo. Its architectural tower replaces the letter O and must never be mirrored in RTL interfaces.

`KORZAH` is the official name in every language. Keep it in Latin characters inside Arabic copy; do not transliterate it or redraw an Arabic wordmark without a separate, professionally approved lettering system.

- Use the full wordmark for covers, authentication, navigation, and formal material.
- Use the tower icon for favicons, compact navigation, avatars, and app launch surfaces.
- Minimum full-wordmark width: 120 px digitally or 32 mm in print.
- Minimum icon size: 20 px digitally or 6 mm in print.
- Clear space: at least the width of the tower's center spine on all sides.
- Approved backgrounds: Obsidian, Graphite, Warm Ivory, or white.
- Never stretch, skew, rotate, recolor individual floors, add glow, or mirror the mark.

## Core colors

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Obsidian | `#090909` | Brand canvas, sidebar, app icon |
| Graphite | `#191918` | Dark surfaces and primary type |
| Construction Gold | `#D5A928` | Primary actions, active states, key data |
| Deep Gold | `#9C6E12` | Accessible light-background accents |
| Warm Ivory | `#F8F7F4` | Main light workspace |
| Concrete | `#DEDAD1` | Dividers and quiet borders |
| Stone | `#736F68` | Secondary copy and metadata |

Gold is a structural signal, not a decorative wash. Use it for one decisive action or state per composition. Status colors remain semantic and must not be replaced with gold.

## Light-mode surface architecture

Light mode uses warm architectural layers rather than pure white and generic gray:

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Canvas | `#F3EFE7` | Application background |
| Surface | `#FBFAF6` | Headers and quiet sections |
| Raised surface | `#FFFDF8` | Cards, forms, menus and dialogs |
| Muted surface | `#EEE8DD` | Hover states, wells and grouped areas |
| Structural line | `#DED4C3` | Card and table separation |
| Strong line | `#C7B9A5` | Form controls and prominent boundaries |
| Light-mode ink | `#211F1B` | Primary text |
| Muted text | `#71695F` | Secondary copy and metadata |

Use the layers in this order: canvas → surface → raised surface. Avoid pure `#FFFFFF` for large product surfaces. Construction Gold remains an action and emphasis color; it must not replace semantic success, warning, or error colors.

## Typography

- **Latin interface and display:** Manrope, weights 400–800.
- **Arabic interface and display:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic, weights 400–700.
- **Technical data:** IBM Plex Mono where a fixed-width treatment materially improves IDs or code.
- Use tabular figures for financial and dashboard values.
- Arabic line-height should remain more generous than Latin; never force the same compact line-height.

## Layout language

- Use strong grids, calm negative space, and clear logical-start alignment.
- Outer surfaces may be soft; inner controls should be tighter and more precise.
- Prefer 8 px control radii and 10–12 px container radii.
- Use thin construction-gold rules sparingly to establish hierarchy.
- Keep charts chronological; page direction must never reverse data meaning.
- In Arabic, mirror layout and directional controls, never the logo or data ownership.

## Image direction

Use monumental geometry, concrete, dark stone, brushed metal, warm architectural light, floor plans, measured linework, and quiet premium interiors. Avoid generic skyline stock photos, blue corporate imagery, glossy gold, keys-in-hand clichés, and fake luxury crests.

## Voice

Korzah speaks clearly and calmly. Copy should be specific, concise, and operational.

- Say “تابع كل فرصة من أول تواصل حتى الإغلاق.”
- Avoid buzzwords, exaggerated claims, and excessive exclamation marks.
- Prefer active verbs and direct error messages.
- Keep English and Arabic equally intentional; Arabic is not secondary copy.

## Motion

- 160–240 ms for controls and navigation.
- Use opacity and transform only for routine transitions.
- A 1 px press movement is preferred over elastic or playful motion.
- Loading and progress may use a measured left-to-right gold line; document RTL must not reverse chronological progress semantics.
